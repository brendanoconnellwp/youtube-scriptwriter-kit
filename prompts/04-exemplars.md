# Step 4: Pick exemplars → skill/youtube-scriptwriter/exemplars/

Choose **3 to 5** of the creator's videos (see `creator.md`) for the skill to read for rhythm. Aim for:
- Strong performers (★ in catalog.md, or high avg % viewed in studio.csv).
- Different formats: at least one tutorial, one explainer/opinion, and one build-along if they have one.
- Recent style over old, if their delivery has changed.
- Complete, good-quality captions.

For each, write `exemplars/<format>-<slug>.md` containing:
1. A 3-line header: title, views, and **why it's here** (what it demonstrates: a great hook, a clean demo flow...).
2. The transcript **with timestamps removed**, lightly cleaned (fix obvious caption mishearings, add paragraph breaks at topic shifts, don't rewrite their words).

Keep each under ~3,000 words. For a longer video, keep the first 4 minutes, one strong middle section and the last 2 minutes, with `[...]` markers.

Update `exemplars/README.md` with a one-line index of the files. Tell the creator which you picked and why, so they can swap any out.
